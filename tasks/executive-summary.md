## Three Critical Findings

### 1. Content Crisis
**87 blog posts → 0 in top 10 pages**

The existing content library is not optimized for search. Posts are:
- Dated (many from 2023)
- Not interlinked
- Missing schema markup
- Not targeting high-value keywords

**Fix:** Content audit + pillar page strategy + refresh program

### 2. Authority Dilution
**Two domains = split SEO power**

| Domain | Users | Problem |
|--------|-------|---------|
| encore-funding.com | 21,280 | Backlinks only help this domain |
| gov.encore-funding.com | 6,877 | Backlinks only help this domain |

If consolidated: **28,000 users strengthening ONE domain**

**Recommendation:** Migrate subdomain to `encore-funding.com/govcon/`

### 3. Paid Search Dependency
**Organic (28.3%) ≈ Paid (24.3%)**

Paying for traffic that could come organically. With proper SEO:
- Organic should be 45%+ of traffic
- Paid search can be reduced by 30-50%
