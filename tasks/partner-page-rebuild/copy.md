## Words to avoid

- "Learn more" (vague — every CTA should say what happens next)
- "Get in touch" (low intent vs. "Apply now")
- "Fan" — current H1 says "GovCon Giants Fan?" — this filters out the buyer who hasn't binge-watched the YouTube channel yet
- "Premier" / "Leading" / "World-class" (filler superlatives)
- Anything that says the user needs to "qualify" before knowing the offer — invert it: tell them what they get, then qualify them via the form
