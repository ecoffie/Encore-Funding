Funding Questions? We Have Answers.

Do you fund prime contractors only, or subs too?

Both. We fund prime contractors and qualifying subcontractors. The key is the underlying federal receivable — if a federal agency is the ultimate payer, we can typically fund it.

How fast can I get my first advance?

Most clients see funding within 48 hours of application approval. Initial review takes 4 to 24 hours. No credit pull is required for the initial review.

What contract types qualify?

Firm-Fixed-Price, T&M, Cost-Plus, Schedule 21A, IDIQ task orders, BPA call orders. If you have a federal contract and an invoice you can issue against it, we can fund it.

Will you need a personal guarantee?

No. We fund against the federal receivable, not against you personally. That's the entire point of our model — we underwrite the contract, not your credit score.

What does the fee structure look like?

Transparent, contract-by-contract pricing based on invoice volume and federal payment cycle. We quote upfront with no hidden fees. Talk to our team for a same-day quote on your specific situation.
