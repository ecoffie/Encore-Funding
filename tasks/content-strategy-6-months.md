## Content Production Specs

### Blog Post Template
1. Meta title (under 60 chars, include keyword)
2. Meta description (under 160 chars, include CTA)
3. H1 heading (match intent, include keyword)
4. Introduction (100-150 words, hook reader)
5. Table of contents (for posts 1,500+ words)
6. 3-5 H2 sections with keyword variations
7. Images (minimum 2, with descriptive alt text)
8. Internal links (minimum 3)
9. External links (1-2 authoritative sources)
10. CTA section (clear next step)
11. Author bio

### Pillar Page Template
1. All blog post requirements PLUS:
2. Table of contents with jump links
3. 3,000+ words
4. Video embed (where available)
5. Downloadable resource
6. Links to all cluster content
7. FAQ section with schema
8. Custom graphics/infographics

### Case Study Template
1. Client challenge (what problem?)
2. Encore solution (what did we do?)
3. Results (specific metrics)
4. Client quote
5. 800-1,000 words
6. Before/after comparison
7. Clear CTA
