## LinkedIn Strategy (Dual Audience)

### Why LinkedIn Matters
GovCon decision-makers and Staffing entrepreneurs both live on LinkedIn. While SEO builds organic discovery, LinkedIn reaches **established business owners ready for funding conversations**.

### Dual Audience Approach
Encore serves **Staffing** and **GovCon** audiences. By alternating content:
- Each segment sees only ~2 relevant posts/week — avoiding fatigue
- Total output of 12-16 posts/month stays under the fatigue threshold
- Use LinkedIn's organic targeting to segment posts by industry

### LinkedIn + SEO Integration
- **Content Amplification:** Every blog post/landing page gets LinkedIn distribution
- **Authority Signals:** LinkedIn engagement builds social proof that reinforces SEO credibility
- **Direct Lead Generation:** LinkedIn posts drive traffic to landing pages
- **Backlink Opportunities:** LinkedIn visibility leads to guest post invitations and mentions
