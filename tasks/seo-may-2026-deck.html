Plain-English Glossary Reference

A few search-marketing terms, in everyday language — no SEO background needed

Impression
Your page showed up in someone's Google results. They saw it — may or may not have clicked.
Click-Through Rate (CTR)
Of the people who saw you in Google, the % who actually clicked. 100 saw it, 9 clicked = 9% CTR.
Ranking / Position
Where you show up. Position 1 = top result; "page one" = first 10. Higher = far more clicks.
Indexed
Google found and filed your page so it can show in searches. Not indexed = invisible.
Organic traffic
Visitors who found you through a free Google search — not from paid ads.
Conversion / Key Event
A visitor took the action you want — here, submitting a funding application.
Engagement rate
% of visitors who actually did something instead of bouncing. Higher = better-quality traffic.
Schema
Hidden labels in your code telling Google "this is an article / a business" — earns richer listings.
Sitemap
A list of all your pages you hand to Google so it knows what to find and file.
Search Console
Google's free tool showing how you perform in search — searches, rankings, clicks.
