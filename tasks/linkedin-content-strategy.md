## Executive Summary

Leadership concern about appearing "spammy" with daily posting is **well-founded and supported by data**. However, with two distinct audiences (Staffing + GovCon), the optimal strategy is **3-4 posts per week** — alternating between audiences so each segment sees only ~2 relevant posts weekly.

### The Bottom Line

| Metric | Daily Posting (Rejected) | Recommended Strategy |
|--------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| Posts/Week | 5-7 | **3-4** |
| Monthly Volume | 20-30 | **12-16** |
| Content Split | Mixed | **50% Staffing / 50% GovCon** |
| Audience Targeting | None | **By Industry** |
| Risk | Audience fatigue | Each audience sees ~2 posts/week |

## Part 1: The Dual-Audience Advantage

### Why Two Audiences Changes the Math

Encore serves **Staffing** and **GovCon** audiences. By alternating content:
- Each segment sees only ~2 relevant posts/week — avoiding fatigue
- Total output doubles while staying under the 15/month fatigue threshold
- Competitor **Parabilis** (3,949 followers) posts 2-3x/week for GovCon only
- Encore can match GovCon presence while also serving Staffing

### LinkedIn's Hidden Targeting Feature

[87% of B2B companies don't know this exists](https://ligosocial.com/blog/linkedin-company-page-audience-targeting-the-hidden-feature-most-brands-ignore) — you can target company page posts to specific segments:
- Target by industry (Staffing Services vs Defense/Government)
- Target by job function
- GovCon posts only go to GovCon followers
- Staffing posts only go to Staffing followers

**This lets you post 4x/week without fatiguing either audience.**

## Part 2: What the Data Says

### Optimal Posting Frequency (B2B Company Pages)

Research from [Buffer's study of 2M+ LinkedIn posts](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin/) and [industry benchmarks](https://ligo.ertiqah.com/blog/linkedin-company-page-posting-frequency-what-actually-works-in-2025) reveals:

| Frequency | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| 2-5 posts/week | +1,000 impressions per post |
| 8-12 posts/month | **Maximum qualified leads for B2B** |
| 12-15 posts/month | Audience fatigue threshold begins |
| 15+ posts/month | Engagement per post **declines** |

**Key Finding:** With dual audiences and proper targeting, 12-16 posts/month means each audience sees only 6-8 posts — well within the optimal zone.

### Why Daily Posting Can Hurt Performance

1. **Content Cannibalization**: LinkedIn's algorithm now extends high-performing post lifespans to [48-72 hours](https://www.beamio.io/blogs/linkedin-posting-frequency-optimal-automation-2026). Posting new content while previous content is gaining traction triggers a "volume tax."

2. **Quality Signal Degradation**: [2026 algorithm changes](https://www.dowsocial.com/linkedin-algorithm-2026/) reward dwell time (61+ seconds = 15.6% engagement) over volume. Rushed daily content gets scrolled past (1.2% engagement).

3. **Audience Fatigue**: Research shows the fatigue threshold is [12-15 posts monthly](https://ligo.ertiqah.com/blog/linkedin-company-page-posting-frequency-what-actually-works-in-2025). With targeting, each audience stays under this.

## Part 4: Recommended Strategy

### Posting Schedule (Dual Audience)

**Recommendation: 3-4 posts per week (12-16 per month)**

| Day | Audience | Content Type |
|-----|----------|--------------|
| Tuesday | **Staffing** | Payroll funding, cash flow, growth tips |
| Wednesday | **GovCon** | Set-asides, compliance, contract financing |
| Thursday | **Staffing** | Back-office, industry trends |
| Friday (optional) | **GovCon** | GCG partnership content, case studies |

**Why this schedule:**
- Each audience sees only ~2 posts/week
- [Best engagement days](https://hashmeta.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-linkedin-for-maximum-b2b-engagement-data-driven-strategy-guide/) are Tuesday-Thursday
- 24+ hours between posts prevents cannibalization
- Matches competitor Parabilis cadence on GovCon side

### Content Mix by Audience

**Staffing Content (50%):**
- Payroll funding challenges and solutions
- Cash flow management for staffing firms
- Back-office technology and efficiency
- Growth strategies for staffing entrepreneurs
- Client success stories (staffing)

**GovCon Content (50%):**
- Set-aside certifications (8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, HUBZone)
- Assignment of Claims education
- Contract financing explainers
- GovCon Giants partnership content
- Recompete Tracker insights
- Client success stories (GovCon)

**Within each audience, apply the 50/30/20 rule:**
- 50% Educational (guides, tips, data)
- 30% Authority (thought leadership, partnerships)
- 20% Direct (client success, CTAs)

### Content Formats That Work

| Format | Performance | Use For |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Carousels | 11.2x more impressions vs text | Guides, checklists, step-by-step |
| Video (vertical) | 71% more impressions | Quick tips, expert interviews |
| Native articles | Higher dwell time | Deep dives, pillar content |
| Text + image | Baseline | Quick updates, commentary |

**Avoid:** External links in posts (kills reach). Use "link in comments" or zero-click content.

## Part 7: Addressing the "Spammy" Concern

### Why 3-4x/Week Is NOT Spammy

1. **Dual-Audience Split**: Each audience sees only ~2 posts/week
2. **Industry Standard**: Parabilis posts 2-3x/week for one audience
3. **Data-Backed**: Research shows 12-16 posts/month is sustainable with targeting
4. **Algorithm-Aligned**: Prevents cannibalization with 24+ hours between posts
5. **Professional Expectation**: B2B audiences expect regular thought leadership

### What WOULD Be Spammy

- Same audience seeing daily posts
- Multiple posts per day
- Pure promotional content without value
- Repetitive sales messaging
- Engagement bait ("Like if you agree!")

### The Credibility Framework

Per [Mark Amtower](https://www.linkedin.com/in/markamtower), GovCon's top LinkedIn authority:

> "In GovCon, you don't need millions of followers — you need a direct 'why factor.' Your tribe might only number in the hundreds, but those are the only ones you need to influence."

## Part 8: Employee Advocacy (Force Multiplier)

### The Data

- Personal profiles generate [8x more engagement](https://lagrowthmachine.com/linkedin-marketing-strategy-2026/) than company pages
- Users are [3x more likely to trust](https://cxl.com/blog/linkedin-for-b2b-marketing/) individual content vs brand content
- [80% of B2B leads](https://martal.ca/linkedin-statistics-lb/) from social come through LinkedIn

### Recommendation

1. **Identify 3-5 Encore team members** active on LinkedIn
2. **Coordinate content**: They share unique perspectives on company themes
3. **Avoid duplication**: Different angles on same topic, not copy-paste
4. **Amplify company posts**: Team reacts/comments within first hour (boosts reach 20%)

## Part 9: Future Consideration — Showcase Page

If GovCon division grows significantly, consider creating a **LinkedIn Showcase Page**:

- Main Encore Funding page: Staffing content
- Encore GovCon Showcase: GovCon-specific content

**Pros:**
- Cleaner audience segmentation
- Dedicated follower base for each vertical
- Allows higher posting frequency per audience

**Cons:**
- Splits overall audience
- More pages to manage
- Requires consistent content for both

**Recommendation:** Monitor GovCon growth for 90 days. If GovCon content consistently outperforms or GovCon followers grow significantly, evaluate Showcase Page option.
