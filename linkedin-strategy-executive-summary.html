Dual Audience Advantage

Encore serves Staffing and GovCon audiences. By alternating content, each segment sees only ~2 relevant posts/week — avoiding fatigue while doubling total output. Competitor Parabilis (3,949 followers) posts 2-3x/week for GovCon only. Encore can match their GovCon presence while also serving Staffing.

Why Dual-Audience Strategy Works

  • No fatigue: Each audience sees ~2 posts/week (their content only)
  • Hidden feature: LinkedIn's organic targeting segments by industry
  • Competitive parity: Match Parabilis on GovCon + serve Staffing
  • Quality focus: 61+ sec dwell time = 15.6% engagement
